## Automated Technologies International

Industrial measurement, CAD/CAM and robotic manufacturing integration

ATI combines dimensional metrology, reverse engineering and CAD/CAM to support complex manufacturing applications and robotic integration.

Robert R. Francis is ATI’s Founder and President and the originating architect of AMACS, ATI Gateway and AMACS NX.

ATI’s Gateway development is documented in its 2024 Siemens Technology Partner Program Agreement for ATI 6X Additive Integration with NX CAM.
